Research

Evaluating Retrieval-Augmented Generation Strategies for Bangla Biomedical Question Answering

Undergraduate thesis, 2025. Accepted at IEEE FMLDS 2026. Preprint on arXiv

I built and compared several RAG strategies for multiple-choice biomedical question answering in Bangla, using BanglaMedQA and BanglaMMedBench (2,000 questions in total). Each strategy paired a retrieval approach with a large language model, and the study asks how knowledge-grounded generation can make biomedical answers in Bangla more reliable.

89.54% highest accuracy, reached by Agentic RAG, the best of the strategies evaluated
